Happy Five Months

Five months ago, my heart found its favourite place.

Some people arrive like passing seasons, beautiful enough to remember, yet never meant to stay.

You were never a season. You felt like something my soul had misplaced long before I was born.

Meeting you didn't feel like discovering someone new. It felt like remembering someone I had always known.

Sometimes I wonder whether souls recognize each other before eyes ever do. Because mine grew quiet the moment it found you.

I still think about the way your glasses make your gentle doe eyes look even bigger. As though the universe wanted me to fall in love twice.

Whenever you smile, the whole world becomes softer. Not brighter. Softer. Like everything has finally remembered how to breathe.

I think about Chamiers. Not because it was beautiful, but because it became beautiful the moment I walked through it beside you.

And I think about Hocco. Not because of the Golden Nut Cake, but because somewhere between sharing tiny bites and stealing little smiles, I learned what happiness actually tastes like.

People spend their whole lives searching for a place that feels like home. I found mine in your laughter. In your eyes. In your hand. In you.

I know five months is only a page in the story we're writing. But if these pages are already this beautiful, I cannot wait to read the rest with you.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every walk, every hug, every memory, and every ordinary day you transformed into something unforgettable.

Thank you for being my Pozholi, my Cookie, my Potito, my Baby, and forever, the Queen of my Dreams.

Happy Five Months, Shraddha. I love you more quietly than words can ever say, and more deeply than silence could ever keep. Here's to us, and to every page still waiting to be written.
